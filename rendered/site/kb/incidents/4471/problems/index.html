Engineering closed #4471 on 5 October. parser is fixed (fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales). hint will not be fixed: the booking-v3 form is retired. one-day is not covered by either and stays open.

The problems

parser: the day and month are swapped. The shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so their dates come out flipped (7 April becomes 4 July). It comes in through the app, by email and through booking-v2’s form. This is the bug the queued fix is for.

hint: the same swap, made by the form. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. The tenant’s people type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. These reports look like the parser bug, but they come only through booking-v3’s form. The parser fix leaves them broken. The fix is the hint, not the parser. Intake tagged reports by the reporter’s browser, so its hint tag does not match this group: the tenant and the form decide it.

one-day: the date is one day off. These reports show no swap. “Tuesday 12 May” arrives as Monday 11 May; one meeting is Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto; an invite arrives before the booking. That is a time-zone shift, not a day/month order. The desk read these reports this way already (desk-notes.json). The desk counts them where their tenant files them, under parser, so they show in both rows.

Why they were merged
